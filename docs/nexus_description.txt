[size=4][b]General description
[/b][/size]
This mod adds an [b]Electric Scooter[/b] to the game. It replaces the skateboard when you ride it, and it works with the game's own skating system (jumps, steering, braking).

Features:
[list]
[*]Buy it from the [b]skateboard seller at the Shred Shack[/b] (2500$)[/*]
[*]Below the engage speed, you push with your foot like on a skateboard[/*]
[*]Above it, the [b]electric motor[/b] takes over: keep the forward key pressed and the scooter accelerates by itself while the rider stays still[/*]
[*]Custom 3D model, shown on the ground and in your hand[/*]
[*]Everything is configurable (speed, acceleration, price of the pose, hands, elbows, feet...) and reloadable in game with [b]F9[/b][/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]How to use
[/b][/size]
To use it, simply :
[list]
[*]Go to the [b]Shred Shack[/b] and talk to the skateboard seller[/*]
[*]Purchase the [b]Electric Scooter[/b][/*]
[*]Equip it and ride it like a skateboard[/*]
[*]Reach the engage speed (16 km/h by default), then hold the forward key to let the motor do the work[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]

After the first launch, the settings are in [b]UserData/MelonPreferences.cfg[/b], category [b][ElectricScooter][/b]. Press [b]F9[/b] in game to reload them while riding.
[list]
[*]MotorEnabled: enable or disable the electric assist[/*]
[*]MotorEngageSpeedKmh: speed above which the motor takes over (default 16)[/*]
[*]MotorTopSpeedKmh: top speed of the motor (default 40)[/*]
[*]MotorAcceleration: motor acceleration (default 5)[/*]
[*]Hands, elbows, feet and body offsets to adjust the rider's pose[/*]
[/list]

[size=4]Known issues[/size]

Please beware of the following when using the mod:
[list]
[*]The scooter is not visible by other players in multiplayer yet[/*]
[*]The handlebar does not turn with the steering yet[/*]
[/list]

This has not been tested in multiplayer yet!! Please be aware of possible bugs!
